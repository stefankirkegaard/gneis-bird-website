Can I try Bird before choosing a plan?

Of course. Book a demo and we’ll walk through your workflow, set up a sample project and help you find the right fit.

Can I change plans as my team grows?

Yes. Move between Starter and Team as your needs change. If you’re coordinating multiple teams or a larger portfolio, we’ll help you shape an Enterprise plan.

Do guests need a paid seat?

No. Guest viewers can open shared drawings and follow project updates. Team members who create, edit and manage work need a paid seat.

What happens to finished projects?

Archive completed projects to keep your workspace tidy. Your drawings, decisions and project history stay available when you need them.

Can you help us get our team set up?

Absolutely. Team includes a guided onboarding session. Enterprise adds a tailored rollout, training and a dedicated person to help you along the way.
