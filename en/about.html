Our story

From chaotic updates to
clear, actionable insights

Bird was born on the construction site, shaped by real, everyday challenges.

Grounded in reality

Built by experienced developers working closely with construction professionals and leading Danish contractors, Bird is grounded in reality and designed to work where projects are built.

Instead of chasing scattered updates across emails, calls and meetings, teams get one clear source of truth that helps them spot changes early and keep projects on track.

01

One source of truth

All project updates in one place, so everyone works from the same picture - from the office to the site.

02

Less noise. Faster decisions

Instant answers to what changed, why it matters, and what to do next. Clear next actions, not long reports.

03

Unstructured input, structured data

AI detects changes and risks in everyday updates and shows their timeline impact the moment they happen.
