What is Bird?

Bird is an AI-powered construction management platform that brings all project updates into one clear source of truth, helping teams spot changes early and keep projects on track.

Who is Bird built for?

Bird is built for construction managers, project leaders, site teams and contractors - everyone involved in delivery, from the office to the site.

How does Bird use AI?

Bird turns unstructured input like drawings, notes and site updates into structured data. It detects changes and risks, shows their timeline impact, and answers questions in plain language.

What problems does Bird solve on site?

Scattered updates, missed changes and endless back and forth. Bird keeps every update, drawing revision and decision in one shared place, so teams catch issues early and decide faster.

How fast can we get started?

Most teams are flying with Bird within 48 hours or less. Book a demo and we'll set up your first project with you.
