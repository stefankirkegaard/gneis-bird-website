Where updates go missing

On a typical project, a single change can travel through an email thread, a phone call, a photo in a group chat and a line in the meeting minutes before it reaches the drawing. Every hand-off is a chance for context to drop out, and by the time the change reaches the site team, the reason behind it is often gone.

None of this happens because people are careless. It happens because updates arrive in many formats, from many stakeholders, and nobody has one place where they all come together.

The cost of a missed change

When a revised window dimension never reaches the facade contractor, the problem rarely shows up right away. It shows up weeks later as rework, a delayed delivery or a tense coordination meeting. The later a change is caught, the more it costs to fix.

Bird was born on the construction site, shaped by real, everyday challenges.

One clear source of truth

The fix is not more meetings or longer reports. It is a shared place where every update, drawing revision and decision stays linked to the project timeline, so anyone can see what changed, why it matters and what to do next.

  • Live change detection across drawings and documents
  • Timeline impact analysis the moment a change lands
  • Early risk identification before issues reach the site
